# Sponsorship business case

## Executive proposition

MarketShield is a governed decision-support platform for product actuaries and product teams facing competitive market-share pressure.

It connects competitive signal detection, internal performance diagnosis, actuarial response simulation, compliance challenge, human approval, and post-decision measurement.

The proposal is not to fund an autonomous pricing agent. The proposal is to fund a twelve-week controlled pilot that tests whether a cross-functional team can reach a better, more traceable product decision materially faster.

## Business problem

Competitive responses are often slow because product teams must manually combine competitor product material, quote and issue data, adviser feedback, actuarial models, customer-outcome requirements, and committee approvals.

This creates three risks:

- continued loss of profitable new business or assets;
- reflexive price reductions that do not solve the real cause;
- weak evidence, assumptions, or controls under time pressure.

## Proposed workflow

`observe → enrich → diagnose → simulate → govern → approve → measure`

## Illustrative first use case

The demonstration uses a fictional individual life-cover case:

- segment: digitally advised professional lives aged 28–45;
- channel: independent advisers;
- problem: falling quote conversion;
- competitor signal: faster underwriting;
- leading diagnosis: turnaround and adviser workflow friction;
- recommended test: restricted rapid-underwriting pilot rather than a broad price cut.

All current figures are synthetic.

## Value hypotheses

The pilot should test whether MarketShield can:

- reduce committee-pack preparation time by at least 50%;
- detect material competitor changes earlier;
- improve root-cause discipline before product or pricing action;
- create traceable sources, assumptions, model versions, and approvals;
- improve expected-versus-realised outcome measurement.

## Illustrative sponsorship request

The demo uses an indicative envelope of **R2.4 million for twelve weeks**. This is a planning placeholder.

The sponsor should provide:

- an executive sponsor;
- one product and one channel use case;
- a product actuary or product owner;
- an approved analytical sandbox;
- access to a validated actuarial calculation process;
- part-time compliance, legal, risk, security, privacy, data, and distribution participation;
- checkpoints at weeks 4, 8, and 12.

## Go, revise, or stop

Proceed beyond the pilot only when:

1. Business owners accept at least one diagnosis.
2. An approved actuarial tool validates at least one response.
3. Decision preparation is materially faster.
4. Required control functions accept the design.
5. Users choose to open a second case.
6. Expected economic value justifies further investment.

Stop or redesign when evidence is unreliable, actuarial integration is not feasible, cycle time does not improve, or control costs exceed expected value.
