## 0:00–0:45 — Decision problem

“Product teams often detect competitive pressure after sales have already deteriorated. They then spend weeks combining competitor documents, quote data, adviser feedback, actuarial modelling, and governance review. MarketShield compresses that work into a controlled decision workflow.”

## 0:45–1:45 — Command centre

Show the synthetic share decline, premium exposure, conversion decline, and competitor signal.

“MarketShield does not assume price is the problem. It opens a case and tests competing explanations.”

Run the agent analysis.

## 1:45–3:00 — Diagnosis

Show underwriting turnaround and adviser workflow friction as the leading causes.

“The evidence suggests that a broad premium cut could sacrifice margin without solving the main customer and adviser problem.”

Show the evidence ledger and blocked non-public competitor information.

## 3:00–4:45 — Scenario lab

Evaluate the rapid-underwriting option.

“The language model is not the actuarial model. In production, approved actuarial tools calculate the economics while MarketShield orchestrates, compares, and documents the result.”

Discuss conversion, VNB, margin, capital, claims risk, and fairness review.

## 4:45–5:45 — Governance

“This system recommends. It does not silently change a premium, decline cover, issue advice, or launch a campaign.”

Show customer-outcome, privacy, competition-information, model-validation, and human approval controls.

## 5:45–7:15 — Sponsorship request

“We are not asking for an enterprise transformation. We are asking for a twelve-week proof on one product and one channel.”

Illustrative request:

- R2.4 million planning envelope;
- controlled analytical sandbox;
- product actuary or product owner;
- actuarial model access;
- compliance, legal, risk, data, security, and distribution participation;
- checkpoints at weeks 4, 8, and 12.

## 7:15–8:00 — Stop conditions

“The pilot proceeds only if it produces an accepted diagnosis, an actuarially validated response, faster decision preparation, control acceptance, and repeat user demand. Otherwise, we stop or redesign.”

## Likely sponsor questions

### Is this just a dashboard?

No. The dashboard is the demonstration surface. The product is the workflow connecting evidence, internal performance, actuarial tools, controls, approvals, and outcome monitoring.

### Can the AI set prices?

Not in the pilot. It can formulate and compare scenarios, but approved actuarial tools calculate results and authorised humans approve changes.

### How are hallucinations controlled?

Restricted retrieval, source provenance, structured outputs, deterministic tools, confidence thresholds, exception handling, review gates, and audit logs.

### Why Rust?

Rust is useful for concurrent ingestion, parsing, typed orchestration, and efficient long-running services. It does not make model inference itself faster when latency is external.

### What is the biggest delivery risk?

Access to clean funnel data and a controlled actuarial calculation interface. The pilot tests these dependencies early.
