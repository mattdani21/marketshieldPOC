# Governance and regulatory design

This proof of concept is a decision-support service. It is not legal or actuarial advice.

## Operating boundary

MarketShield may collect approved evidence, analyse approved aggregates, generate hypotheses, call approved tools, compare scenarios, draft materials, and record approvals.

It must not independently set premiums or benefits, accept or decline cover, issue regulated advice, make a solely automated material customer decision, launch a campaign, or modify production insurer systems.

## Customer outcomes

Each response should assess target market, customer need, value, affordability, exclusions, communication, advice implications, service, claims, complaints, lapses, withdrawals, vulnerable customers, and post-launch monitoring.

## POPIA and privacy

Use aggregated or pseudonymised data by default. Controls must cover lawful purpose, minimisation, access, retention, human review, cross-border processing, operators, source lineage, and incidents.

Sensitive health or underwriting data must not be used for market diagnosis without a specific, necessary, lawful, and approved purpose.

## Competition information

Classify evidence as public, licensed, institution-owned, authorised research, restricted, or prohibited. Block non-public competitively sensitive information. Record source, dates, licence, hash, extracted fact, verification, confidence, and expiry.

## Actuarial and model governance

The language model is not the actuarial engine. Real decisions must use approved tools and record model version, basis, input contract, run ID, results, validation state, exceptions, overrides, and approvals.

## Human accountability

Material recommendations may require product actuarial, underwriting, distribution, compliance, legal, risk, model validation, privacy, security, and committee approval.

## Outsourcing and audit

Third parties require materiality, data, subcontractor, audit-access, resilience, incident, intellectual-property, exit, and deletion assessments.

Production audit design must be tamper-evident and record sources, model calls, tool runs, controls, approvals, overrides, actions, expected outcomes, realised outcomes, and incidents.
